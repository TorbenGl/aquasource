# FathomNet (`fathomnet`)
Status: researched · researched 2026-10-06

## Summary
FathomNet is MBARI's open database of annotated ocean images (481,126 images on 2026-10-08, 17 contributing institutions, REST API with no login for reads). The image licence is stored per image in the field `imageLicense`, and it is dominated by non-commercial terms: CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0 240,912 images (50.1 %) and CC-BY-NC-4.0 635 are **Excluded**, CC-BY-4.0 7,948 (1.7 %) and CC0-1.0 231,631 (48.1 %) are usable. The usable part is small in practice. The 7,948 CC-BY images are almost all CENCOOS/MBARI "Planktivore" 224x224 plankton crops with no coordinates (only 16 CC-BY images, from POSCO, have a position: macroalgae scenes off Korea, 4 at 37.469 N 130.828 E near Ulleungdo and 12 at 35.935 N 129.537 E near Pohang). The 231,631 CC0 images are all NOAA NMFS SEFSC (Mississippi Labs) reef-fish drop-camera video frames from the Gulf of Mexico (24.5-30.2 N, 83.1-96.99 W), 100 % with lat/lon and depth, but the coordinate is one per video station copied to every frame, so the precision is **station**, not image. Verdict: **ingest only `imageLicense` = CC0-1.0 (tier A, station-level geo, about 230 k very redundant frames) plus the 16 geolocated CC-BY frames (tier B); drop everything else.** The seed idea "keep CC0 + CC BY, confirm commercial terms with MBARI" is right in outline but the CC BY share is tiny, and a human step is not needed for the CC0 part.

## Entry points
- Web UI: https://database.fathomnet.org/fathomnet/ (Vue app). Site, terms and data-use policy: https://www.fathomnet.org/, https://www.fathomnet.org/terms (last updated 2025-01-12), https://www.fathomnet.org/datause
- REST API base: **`https://database.fathomnet.org/api`** (not `.../fathomnet/api`, which 404s). There is no OpenAPI/Swagger page. Routes were read from the fathomnet-py 1.10.2 source (https://pypi.org/project/fathomnet/, docs https://fathomnet-py.readthedocs.io) and the web app bundle, then tested:
  - `GET /images/count` -> `{"count":481126,"objectType":"ImageEntity"}`
  - `GET /images/list/all?size=&page=&sort=uuid` -> paged full image DTOs (with all bounding boxes; 2 KB-10 KB each)
  - `GET /geoimages?size=&page=&sort=uuid` -> paged **light** records wrapped as `{"content":[...],"pageNumber","pageSize","totalItems":481126,"totalPages":161}` (verified: the records are under `content`, not a bare list) (`uuid, url, latitude, longitude, depthMeters, timestamp, imageLicense, valid, contributorsEmail, lastValidation`), about 0.4 KB each
  - `POST /geoimages/query` and `POST /images/query` (JSON body "GeoImageConstraints") and `POST /geoimages/count`
  - `GET /licenses/list/all` (77 SPDX licences with flags), `GET /imagesetuploads/list/all?page=&size=` (350 uploads with a `darwinCore` block), `GET /stats/list/contributions`, `GET /images/list/imagingtypes` (`Drop Camera`, `ROV`), `GET /images/list/contributors`, `GET /boundingboxes/...`, `GET /taxa/...`
- Python client: `pip install fathomnet` (fathomnet-py 1.10.2, https://github.com/fathomnet/fathomnet-py). Its DTOs do **not** declare `imageLicense`, `annotationLicense` or `imageLicenses` yet, so use raw HTTP for licence filtering.
- Data paper: Katija K. et al. (2022) FathomNet: A global image database for enabling artificial intelligence in the ocean. Scientific Reports 12, 15914. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-19939-2 (CC BY, Crossref). Earlier arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.14646
- Source of the CC0 frames: NOAA NMFS SEFSC "Reef Fish Videos" / "SEAMAP Reef Fish Survey Videos" (InPort https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/30062 and https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/29502), hosted in the public bucket `gs://nmfs_odp_hq/nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/` (HTTP base `https://storage.googleapis.com/nmfs_odp_hq/nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/`).

  - The corpus is live and grows (84 k images in the 2022 paper, 481 k now; the SEFSC set was added 2026-02-03 and updated 2026-09). Record the access date.

## Media
- Types: still images only. The DB stores URLs, not files: the images stay on the contributors' hosts. The CC0 images are JPEG frames cut from reef-fish video at **5 frames per second** (names like `<video>.mp4.00.00.11.800000.jpg` = video time HH.MM.SS.ffffff), so neighbouring frames are near-duplicates.
- Counts (2026-10-08): 481,126 images overall; **CC0 231,631** (168,386 frames come from `.avi` sources, 63,245 from `.mp4`); CC-BY 7,948 (of which 16 georeferenced); Excluded 241,547. Sizes: 1920x1080 or 1920x1200 px, 345 kB-643 kB per JPEG (3 sampled), so about 115 GB for all CC0 frames (estimate). Annotated: each image carries `boundingBoxes` with Latin `concept` names (e.g. "Holacanthus bermudensis", "Lutjanus synagris") and `annotationLicense`.
- Video: the bucket folder also holds the source **140 `.mp4` files (1.21 GB in total, `nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/<video>.mp4`, 25-63 MB each)**, one `<video>.kwcoco.json` annotation file each (153 json), and the 120 MB annotation CSV `gfisher_fathomnet.csv`. The 526 frame folders therefore outnumber the mp4 files: the `.avi` videos (the `SC*-camera*_MM-DD-YY_...` rigs and others) are **not** in the bucket, only their frames. Videos are NOAA material that can be used with the same station coordinate, but they are not in the FathomNet DB itself.
- Time range: `timestamp` is filled for about half of the CC0 frames (114,283 of 231,631 listed rows; all from `SC*-camera*` stereo-rig videos, 2021-03-12 to 2021-04-06); the other videos (names `<9 digits>_cam<n>[_k]`, `2021_NC?-???x`) have `timestamp` null. 73 folder names carry the year 2021 (explicit `2021_` prefix or `-21_` date); the year of the 368 nine-digit-station videos is unknown. The wider SEAMAP survey runs 1992-present (InPort). Plan for "2021-ish, undated".
- Imaging types in the whole DB: `Drop Camera` (231,631 = exactly the CC0 SEFSC set), `ROV` (101,604, all NC-ND or NC), null otherwise (the CC-BY images).
- Filters needed:
  - `valid` must be true. Also drop images whose `url` returns 404 (contributors can remove them).
  - The CC-BY 224x224 plankton ROI crops (CENCOOS, `oer.hpc.msstate.edu/FathomNet/staging/FN26...png`, `width`=`height`=224, no coordinates) are not scene images: drop them (they fail the geo gate anyway).
  - Deployment/retrieval frames (surface, deck, black) are possible in drop-camera video, but every DB frame is an annotated one (the annotation CSV lists fish boxes per frame), so black frames should be rare. I did not look at pixels (no image download); run the usual black/aerial filter.
  - Near-duplicates: 5 fps frames (see the sampling rule), and stereo/multi-camera views of the same station (`cam1..cam5`, `SC1..SC4-camera*`) that share one coordinate.
  - Overlap with other keys: dedup by `sha256` (given per image) or `url`. BenthicNet-1M contains FathomNet sets (NC-ND, excluded there). The SEFSC frames are new in 2026 and not in BenthicNet. The candidate `noaa_fisheries_nodd` (bucket `nmfs_odp_sefsc`, `REEF_FISH` machine-learning data) may hold the same or related SEFSC footage under another path; dedup by video name and `sha256`.

## Geolocation
- Precision level(s) and approximate share:
  - **CC0 SEFSC (48.1 % of the DB, the only large usable part): `station`, 100 % have lat/lon and depth.** From 197,180 CC0 frames listed, **every video folder has exactly one (lat, lon, depth) triple** (441 folders; 0 with more than one) and only 95 distinct coordinates occur, i.e. the coordinate is the deployment position of the camera array, copied to all frames and to all 4-5 camera views (`cam1..cam5`) of the drop. Max 20,154 frames share one coordinate (28.6365 N, 89.5522 W). Coordinates have 4-6 decimals (about 10 m), which is a rounding, not an accuracy.
  - CC-BY (1.7 % of the DB): 16 POSCO images are georeferenced, at **two** positions (4 images at 37.469 N, 130.828 E, upload folder `FN251128095328`, off the west coast of Ulleungdo, East Sea; 12 images at 35.935 N, 129.537 E, folders `FN251128095808` and `FN251128100216`, near Pohang; depth null; verified 2026-10-08) -> `station`; 7,932 CC-BY images (7,892 CENCOOS Planktivore crops plus 40 from the `test`/`test2` uploads) have **no** coordinates -> `none`.
  - Excluded sets (for information): ROV sets from SOI/MBARI/NOAA OER carry per-frame vehicle positions plus CTD fields (`salinity`, `temperatureCelsius`, `oxygenMlL`, `pressureDbar`, e.g. SOI lat 44.38875088 / 44.38907556 for frames 8.4 min apart), 97.2 % of the DB (467,589 of 481,126) has coordinates.
- geo_source (exact field names / table): JSON fields `latitude`, `longitude` (WGS 84 decimal degrees, signed: west and south negative), `depthMeters` (metres, positive down), optional `timestamp` (ISO 8601 UTC), also `altitude`, `pressureDbar` (null for SEFSC), in the records of `POST /api/geoimages/query`, `POST /api/images/query`, `GET /api/geoimages` and `GET /api/images/list/all`. Filter parameters use `minLatitude/maxLatitude/minLongitude/maxLongitude/minDepth/maxDepth`. No separate CRS field (assume WGS 84). Whether `depthMeters` of the SEFSC records is the camera depth or the bottom depth at the station is not stated; it is constant per video, so treat it as station depth.
- How a sample maps to its coordinate: the coordinate is a field of the image record itself (key `uuid`, also `url` and `sha256`). For the CC0 set the video id is the folder part of the `url`: `.../gfisher/<video>/<video>.<mp4|avi>.HH.MM.SS.ffffff.jpg`; all frames of a folder have the same triple.
- Uncertainty: for `station` records, 200 m is an **assumption** (vessel position at deployment of a camera rig, no uncertainty published; rounding is only about 10 m). State this in `geo_uncertainty_m`. For the POSCO CC-BY images use 2000 m (the position is shared by all images of one upload folder and has 3 decimals, about 100 m; basis is unverified; both points lie within about 2 km of the coast, Nominatim puts a hamlet 1.4 km from the Ulleungdo point, so one of them may even be marginally on land).

### resolve_geo recipe
Input: one JSON record (a `geoimages/query` or `images/query` item, see `tests/fixtures/fathomnet/api_geoimages_query.json`, `api_images_query.json`).
1. Licence gate (before anything else): `lic = rec.get("imageLicense")`. If `lic` is `CC0-1.0` -> tier A; `CC-BY-4.0` (or another B id from the table above) -> B; `CC-BY-SA-4.0` -> C; if it contains `NC` or `ND` -> Excluded, return `None` (the sample is dropped); anything else or null -> U (do not ingest). If `rec.get("valid") is False` -> drop.
2. Read `lat = rec.get("latitude")`, `lon = rec.get("longitude")`. If either is None, or not -90 <= lat <= 90 and -180 <= lon <= 180, or both are exactly 0: return `lat=None, lon=None, depth_m=rec.get("depthMeters"), geo_precision="none", geo_source="fathomnet API latitude/longitude (null)", geo_inferred=False, geo_uncertainty_m=None`. (Example: the CC-BY plankton crop `36423e84-a910-4a0f-b616-e643c9255b26`.)
3. `depth_m = rec.get("depthMeters")` as float, or None if null (POSCO records have null depth; SEFSC records always have a value, 19.7-151.1 m). Do not use `pressureDbar`/`altitude`.
4. Precision: if `rec["url"]` starts with `https://storage.googleapis.com/nmfs_odp_hq/nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/` (NOAA SEFSC drop-camera frames) -> `geo_precision="station"`, `geo_inferred=True` (one deployment coordinate copied to all frames of the video folder), `geo_uncertainty_m=200` (assumption, see above). Otherwise (e.g. `oer.hpc.msstate.edu` CC-BY images from POSCO) -> `geo_precision="station"`, `geo_inferred=True`, `geo_uncertainty_m=2000` (assumption: the coordinate is shared by the whole upload folder; these 16 are 5520x3680 / 6720x4480 DSLR-style photos of kelp and other macroalgae (Ecklonia cava, Eisenia bicyclis, Sargassum) that may be intertidal or above-water, apply the above-water filter). Generic ROV/other sources from the Excluded licences would be `image` / `geo_inferred=False` / `None`, but they never reach this step.
5. `geo_source`: `"fathomnet API /geoimages/query (or /images/query) fields latitude, longitude, depthMeters; coordinate is per video deployment (shared by all frames of the folder)"`.
6. Return `(lat, lon, depth_m, geo_precision, geo_source, geo_inferred, geo_uncertainty_m)`. Example, fixture record 1 of `api_geoimages_query.json` (`dcbfc174-...`, `761901231_Cam5.mp4...2.400000.jpg`): `(29.4648, -87.6247, 63.7, "station", <source above>, True, 200)`. Example, record 5 (`ead1a05d-...`, CC-BY-4.0): `(37.469, 130.828, None, "station", ..., True, 2000)`.
7. Optional stronger step used at ingest (needs several records): group the CC0 records by `url.rsplit("/",1)[0]` and check that they share one triple; if a folder ever has several triples, switch that folder to `image` precision with `geo_inferred=False` and `geo_uncertainty_m=None`.

## Access & download recipe
- Enumeration (endpoints / listings / pagination), auth, rate limits:
  - **No auth** for any `GET` or read-only `POST` (writes need a key, not used). No rate limit is documented; I made about 250 calls at 1 request per 0.8-1.5 s with no 429. The proxy path gave occasional `Connection reset` / `SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL` (about 1 call in 15): retry with backoff (3 s, 6 s, ...). Use the project User-Agent, no email address.
  - **Do not page `POST /geoimages/query` or `/images/query` with `offset` over a broad filter**: the order is not deterministic. Two identical calls (`{"imageLicenses":["CC0-1.0"],"limit":2000,"offset":100000}`) returned two completely disjoint sets, and a first naive pass of 78 pages x 3000 gave 231,631 rows but only 197,180 unique `uuid`. Limits above 3000 are accepted (30,000 worked, 8.9 MB).
  - **Stable enumeration option 1**: `GET /geoimages?size=3000&page=N&sort=uuid` (N = 0..160; 3000 light records ~1.2 MB, ~1 s; pages 10 and 11 had zero overlap and the identical request was repeatable). Filter client-side on `imageLicense` (CC0 and CC-BY) and `valid`; about 160 calls cover all 481,126 images (~190 MB of metadata). `GET /images/list/all?...&sort=uuid` has the same stable order but 5-25 times more bytes (boxes).
  - **Option 2** (narrow slices, one page each): `POST /geoimages/query` with `imageLicenses`, a small lat/lon box around a known station and `limit` 30000, so that the answer fits in one response (offset 0 only). `ownerInstitutionCodes` (e.g. `["NOAA NMFS SEFSC"]`), `imagingTypes` (`["Drop Camera"]`), `startTimestamp`/`endTimestamp` and depth limits are also filters. `POST /geoimages/count` with the same body is cheap and tells you the size first.
  - Counting a licence is exact: `POST /geoimages/count` with `{"imageLicenses":["CC0-1.0"]}` -> 231,631.
  - Annotations: `POST /images/query` returns `boundingBoxes` (x, y, width, height in pixels, `concept`, `reviewState`, `annotationLicense`); the per-upload CSV zips (`/static/archive/...csv.zip`, ~0.5 MB each) and `gs://.../gfisher_fathomnet.csv` hold the same boxes without coordinates.
- How to fetch one media file: HTTPS `GET <url>` from the record. For CC0 frames the host is the public GCS bucket (`HEAD` gives 200, `Range: bytes=0-1023` gives 206, `cache-control: public, max-age=3600`, ~345-643 kB each). No auth; GCS supports Range/resume. The source mp4 is at `https://storage.googleapis.com/nmfs_odp_hq/nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/<video>.mp4` (only for the 140 mp4 videos). Verify `sha256` of the download against the record. Other hosts seen (all Excluded or crops): `d2l7vcm2vanphr.cloudfront.net`, `database.fathomnet.org/static/...`, `ws.oncs.ca`, `hurlimage.soest.hawaii.edu`, `fathomnet.s3-us-east-2.amazonaws.com`, `oer.hpc.msstate.edu`.
- Bucket listing (anonymous): `https://storage.googleapis.com/storage/v1/b/nmfs_odp_hq/o?prefix=nodd_tools/datasets/gfisher/&delimiter=/&maxResults=1000&fields=nextPageToken,prefixes,items(name,size)` gives 526 video folders, 140 mp4, 153 kwcoco json, 1 csv.
- Sampling strategy for a budget of N samples (diversity across sites and time):
  1. Enumerate with option 1 and keep `imageLicense in {CC0-1.0, CC-BY-4.0}`, `valid`, and non-null lat/lon (so effectively CC0 plus 16 POSCO images).
  2. Group by station = distinct `(latitude, longitude)` (95 for CC0) and, inside it, by video folder (441+ folders; 526 in the bucket).
  3. Round-robin over stations (shuffle the station order with a fixed seed), then over videos in the station, taking at most 3-5 frames per video and at least 10 s apart (frame names carry the time offset, 0.2 s steps, so keep every 50th frame or more). With N = 5000 this gives about 1000 videos-worth of spread, i.e. every station and video is covered. Cap any station at about 2 % of N (one station has 20 k frames).
  4. Prefer frames with at least one box (all DB frames have boxes) and optionally weight rare `concept`s.
  5. Spread over time with `timestamp` where present (2021-03-12 .. 2021-04-06); the remaining videos are undated.
  6. Never request more than about 1 call per second; images go through the bucket (Google), not through the FathomNet server.

## Short download instruction
1. `GET https://database.fathomnet.org/api/geoimages?size=3000&page=N&sort=uuid` for N = 0.. until empty (stable paging, no auth, ~1 s apart, retry on connection reset).
2. Keep `valid` records with `imageLicense` = `CC0-1.0` (tier A; NOAA SEFSC Gulf of Mexico frames) or `CC-BY-4.0` (tier B) and non-null `latitude`/`longitude`; drop all `NC`/`ND` records.

## Open questions / risks
- Terms of Use say registration is required to use the Database, yet the API is open: is anonymous bulk use acceptable? (Soft risk; the images themselves are CC0 and hosted by NOAA on Google Cloud.)
- The "Benevolent Use" (UN SDG) and acknowledgement conditions of the Data Use Policy are not part of CC0; decide whether to put them in the dataset card.
- Station coordinate uncertainty (200 m) and the meaning of `depthMeters` for SEFSC (camera depth vs bottom depth) are assumptions; nothing in the API or on InPort states them. The paper of the SEAMAP survey (not read) might. Videos with no `timestamp` (about half) have no date.
- Heavy redundancy: 5 fps frames of ~441-526 videos at ~95 stations. Real diversity is far below 231 k.
