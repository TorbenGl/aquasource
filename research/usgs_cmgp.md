# USGS Coastal and Marine Geology Program Video and Photograph Portal (`usgs_cmgp`)
Status: researched · researched 2026-10-06

## Summary
The CMGP Video and Photograph Portal (DOI 10.5066/F7JH3J7N) is a USGS data release that publishes seafloor video and still photos from USGS surveys (California, Oregon, Puget Sound, Alaska, Hawaii/Pacific, Massachusetts, Rhode Island/Long Island Sound, Delmarva, Florida, American Samoa, Crater Lake), plus a large set of above-water oblique aerial coastline imagery that must be filtered out. The seed's Cloudflare worry did not materialise: the DOI now redirects to https://video.ioos.us/ (Axiom Data Science portal 42), and all the data services behind it (GeoServer WFS, a JSON frame service, a photo server) answer plain scripted requests with no auth and no challenge. Underwater content is about 2.5 M navigation rows (one row per 2–10 s of video, plus rows for about 120 k stills), more than 1,000 h of video and more than 160 k photos in total. Licence is tier A: the record is "marked with CC0 1.0 Universal" and the FGDC use constraint says USGS data are in the public domain. Geolocation is excellent: every navigation row and every still has its own lat/lon, so precision is `image` for stills and `segment`/`image` for video frames (the frame is a second offset into the video). The catch is media access: stills are plain JPEGs on a legacy Axiom photo server, but the videos exist only as YouTube uploads (channel "CMG Video"), so fetching them is a human decision about YouTube terms. Verdict: ingest the stills now (tier A, image), and the videos once a human approves the YouTube route.

- Tier: **A** (US-government work, CC0 / public domain). Applies to USGS-authored layers only. Non-USGS layers in the same portal (for example the CSU Monterey Bay "California Undersea Imagery Archive") are not covered: keep them out (tier U) unless checked.

## Media
- Types, counts, formats, resolution, total size, time range:
  - **Video**: more than 4,000 video files and more than 1,001 h in 2016 (Methods summary: "As of 2016, the portal contains more than 163,000 images, over 4,000 video files, and more than 1,001 hours of video covering more than 41,000 km"; this includes aerial). Videos are re-encoded MPEG-4/MP4 for YouTube (SD tape-era footage, some HD), usually with a burned-in text overlay (survey ID, date, station; sometimes position). CA sled surveys have two YouTube IDs per tape: `vertical` and `oblique` camera (`youtube_ids` in the neighborhoodFrames response); WFS carries only the main `youtube_id`. Size unknown (hundreds of GB at YouTube quality).
  - **Stills**: JPEG from Canon EOS-class cameras (the inspected file: 5184x3456, 3.56 MB, Canon EOS 60D, 2012). Rows with a photo id (`picasa_id` not null and not empty): California 78,341 (`axiom:usgsvideoframe`; the provider says "87,000 photographs"), Hawaii/Pacific `usgs_pacific:videopoints_hw` 28,079, Massachusetts `woodshole_media_points` 8,083, Rhode Island 1,793, Puget Sound 918, old California `california_imagery_17` 415, i.e. about 117,600 seafloor stills; plus 5,945 aerial photos in the Alaska layer. About 0.2–0.4 TB total at 1.3–3.5 MB each (estimate: 117,600 x ~2.5 MB = ~0.3 TB). The photo server ignores HTTP Range (it returned the whole 3.5 MB file on a Range request) and the thumbnail endpoint is fixed at ~300 px.
  - **Navigation rows** (WFS `totalFeatures`, underwater layers only): usgsvideoframe (California SMP) 924,759; glacierbay 434,281; puget_sound 424,745; woodshole 321,984; hw 174,379; woodshole_rhode_island 84,272; california_imagery_17 41,700; fa14607 (Oregon OCS) 36,989; gordaridge 85/86 12,321; fa15001 (Delmarva) 9,185; fa14673/fa15604/fa15627 (Monterey Bay 2014–15) 18,618; fa12024 (Connecticut River) 4,044; fa14604 (American Samoa) 1,824; n106or (Crater Lake) 1,942; 1996–98 CA/MB layers (axiom:*_navjoin) 3,609; juandefuca + l_4_85_wf 697. Total about 2.5 M rows. Pulley Ridge (`2001_010_fa`, WMS only in the portal config) was not counted.
  - Sampling of the nav: modern tapes 2 s (`frame` increments of 2), old tapes 10 s, some layers 1 s; `frame` = "the number of seconds into a video" (FGDC lineage text).
  - **Time range**: 1983 (Juan de Fuca Ridge) to 2016; most sled work 2003–2015. The WFS `usgsvideoframe` table has no date column; dates come from the `date` of photo frames in the neighborhoodFrames service, from the cruise/video id (e.g. `C0212SC` = cruise 02 of 2012, `O-1-00-SC` = 2000) or from the `date`/`dateinfo` columns of other layers (`dateinfo` is an Excel serial day, e.g. 40011.174155 = 2009-07-16).
- Filters needed:
  - **Aerial / above-water**: about 70 of the 111 vector layers are oblique aerial coastline imagery (baseline and post-storm flights 1995–2016). Exclude by layer allowlist (below), not by pixel. Aerial layer names match `*ach*`, `*cch*`, `*baseline*`, `*hurricane*`, `stpete_*`, `charley`, `isabel`, `ida`, `sandy`, `ivan`, `2015_335_fa_mediapoints`, `2015_338_fa`, `2016_316_fa_mediapoints`, `usgs_pacific:videopoints_ak` (Alaska shoreline survey, 71 N).
  - Underwater allowlist (WFS `typeName`): `axiom:usgsvideoframe`, `usgs_imagery:california_imagery_17`, `usgs_pacific:videopoints_puget_sound`, `usgs_pacific:videopoints_hw`, `usgs_imagery:videopoints_glacierbay`, `usgs_imagery:woodshole_media_points`, `usgs_imagery:woodshole_rhode_island_media_points`, `usgs_imagery:videopoints_fa12024`, `..._fa14604`, `..._fa14607`, `..._fa14673`, `..._fa15001`, `..._fa15604`, `..._fa15627`, `..._n106or`, `usgs_imagery:juandefuca`, `..._videopoints_l_4_85_wf_navjoin`, `..._gordaridge_85`, `..._gordaridge_86`, `axiom:j_7_96_mb_navjoin`, `axiom:b_1_98_sc_navjoin`, `axiom:h_3_96_nc_navjoin`, `axiom:h_1_98_nc_navjoin`. The mapping of fa-layers to areas was done from the layer extents and descriptions; verify once.
  - Exclude `axiom:cuia_points`, `axiom:all_transects_no_imagery` (California Undersea Imagery Archive, CSU Monterey Bay, non-USGS, licence not read) and the `csmp:*` layers (maps/habitat polygons, no media). `csmp:seafloor<cruise>_video_observations` are habitat annotations by the CSU Seafloor Mapping Lab, a possible label source but of unknown licence.
  - USGS says it removed deck shots, long water-column sequences and blank/distorted footage before upload, so black frames should be rare. Still filter: sled lifted off the bottom, water column, deck, overlay text (crop or mask the burned-in overlay), laser dots (parallel lasers 10 cm apart in Puget Sound footage).
  - Overlaps: not measured. The same survey could appear in more than one layer (for example `axiom:usgsvideoframe` and the older `usgs_imagery:california_imagery_17`, or the per-cruise `csmp:*` observation layers); deduplicate by (`picasa_album_id`, `picasa_id`) for stills and `youtube_id` for videos. Frame grabs of the same YouTube videos may also exist in other USGS ScienceBase releases (for example Faga'alu Bay frame grabs); dedupe by `youtube_id`/video name.

## Geolocation
- Precision level(s) and approximate share:
  - Stills (about 5% of nav rows, about 117,600 images): **image**. Each still has a navigation row with its own lat/lon, and the same value is written into the EXIF GPS tags (verified: EXIF 36 deg 52.70328' N, 122 deg 1.7532' W = 36.878388, -122.02922 = WFS/neighborhoodFrames row for that photo). The coordinate itself is the research-vessel GPS fix matched by time to the photo, so geo_inferred is false for the database but the position is the ship's.
  - Video frames (about 95% of rows): **segment**, geo_inferred true. A frame you extract at second t is interpolated between the two nav rows around t (`frame` column = seconds into video; spacing 1, 2 or 10 s). Use `image` only if t falls exactly on a nav row.
- geo_source: WFS feature type properties `lat`, `lon` (WGS 84 decimal degrees, EPSG:4326) of `axiom:usgsvideoframe` (and `usgs_imagery:*`, `usgs_pacific:*`) with `frame` (seconds offset) as the time key; for stills also EXIF GPSLatitude/GPSLongitude. **Use `properties.lat/lon`, not `geometry.coordinates` and not `outputFormat=csv`**: both the GeoJSON geometry and the CSV columns are rounded to 4 decimals (about 11 m), while `properties.lat/lon` carry 5–6 decimals (checked on `id` 257: properties 36.822463, -121.865927; geometry 36.8225, -121.8659).
- Depth field: none in any layer (`depth_m` = null). Optionally join with bathymetry later (not source data).
- Uncertainty: provider statement (`portal_layergroup_ca_seafloor.json`): "the location of GPS data points in this portal layers represent the location of the survey vessel, which may have minimal offset from the subject of the imagery on the seafloor, typically on the order of +/- 10 meters depending on the depth and currents encountered during deployment." Use 10 m for shallow sled surveys, 50 m for Gorda/Juan de Fuca/deep cruises (no provider figure; my assumption). The FGDC record states no formal horizontal accuracy assessment was done.
- How a sample maps to its coordinate: WFS row key = (`video`, `frame`) or (`picasa_album_id`, `picasa_id`); `video` is the file/tape name and `youtube_id` points to the YouTube upload; `frame` is the second inside that upload. Keep the row `id` for traceability.
- Quality caveats found: (1) neighborhoodFrames for `C0212SC_Tape65` jumps 3.7 km between frames 2756 and 2758 (2 s) – check consecutive-fix speed (> 5 m/s towed sled is implausible) and drop frames next to jumps; (2) the portal's photo `date` and the EXIF capture time differ by 23 min for the same photo (camera clock offset) but seconds agree; use neither for geolocation, only the row coordinate; (3) the `usgs_pacific:videopoints_hw` layer contains rows far from Hawaii (e.g. cruise `F-9-90-CP`, 8.04 N, -148.83) – keep, but do not assume Hawaiian extent.

### resolve_geo recipe
Input: one WFS feature (`properties`), plus for video frames the extraction time t (seconds into the YouTube video) and the other rows of the same `video` ordered by `frame`.
1. If `properties.lat` and `properties.lon` are not null and -90<=lat<=90 and -180<=lon<=180 (and not both 0): candidate = (lat, lon) from `properties` (never from `geometry` or CSV).
2. Still (`picasa_id` not in {null, ""}): return lat, lon, depth_m=null, geo_precision="image", geo_source="WFS <typeName> properties.lat/lon (row id <id>; also EXIF GPS)", geo_inferred=false, geo_uncertainty_m=10.
3. Video frame at t: find rows r0, r1 of the same `video` (and same `youtube_id`) with r0.frame <= t <= r1.frame.
   - t == r0.frame: return that row as in step 2 but geo_precision="image".
   - r1.frame - r0.frame <= 10: linear interpolation in lat/lon (distances are tiny); geo_precision="segment", geo_inferred=true, geo_source="WFS <typeName> properties.lat/lon interpolated on properties.frame", geo_uncertainty_m=10.
   - gap > 10 s or t outside the table: return lat=lon=null, geo_precision="none" (do not extrapolate).
4. QC: if the implied speed between r0 and r1 exceeds 5 m/s, set geo_precision="none" for frames within the jump (case in the fixture, frames 2756–2758).
5. depth_m is always null. If the row has null lat/lon, return all geo fields null with geo_precision="none"; as a fallback the layer extent (`bbox` in the portal layer record) may give geo_precision="region", geo_inferred=true, geo_source="portal layer extent", geo_uncertainty_m=null.

## Access & download recipe
- Enumeration (no auth, about 1 request/s per host, no rate-limit headers seen; JSON from GeoServer is fast, 1,000 rows in about 2 s):
  1. List layers: `search.axds.co/v2/search?portalId=42&type=layer_group&page=1..16` then `oikos.axds.co/rest/minimal-portal-data?id=42&layerGroupUuid=...&includeLookups=false` to get `ogcName`/`wfsUrl` (or just use the allowlist above).
  2. Page the navigation table: `GET <wfsUrl>?service=WFS&version=1.0.0&request=GetFeature&typeName=<typeName>&outputFormat=application/json&sortBy=id&startIndex=<k>&maxFeatures=1000[&propertyName=id,lat,lon,video,frame,youtube_id,photo,picasa_id,picasa_album_id]`. `totalFeatures` in the response gives the table size. `cql_filter=picasa_id IS NOT NULL AND picasa_id <> ''` returns only stills (use URL-encoding via `curl -G --data-urlencode`).
  3. Optional per-video detail with dates and the two camera IDs: `spatial-imagery.srv.axds.co/<spatial_dataset>/neighborhoodFrames/?frames=60&video=<video>&startFrame=<s>`; `<spatial_dataset>` comes from `configJson.spatial_dataset` (e.g. `seafloor`, `woodshole`, `glacierbay`, `usgs_pacific_puget_sound`).
- Fetch one still: `GET https://servomatic9000.axiomalaska.com/photo-server/usgs/<picasa_album_id>/<picasa_id>/photo?` with a normal User-Agent (HTTP 200, `content-type: image/jpeg`, ETag/Last-Modified present, **no range/resume support**: retry the whole file). The photo-server ids are legacy Picasa Web Albums ids hosted by Axiom; the server is the old "servomatic9000" host and may disappear.
- Fetch video: no direct file URL. The only copy is the YouTube upload (`youtube_id`). A scripted route would be `yt-dlp https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>` with `--download-sections` (not tested by me, and subject to YouTube's terms; a first scripted watch-page request already got HTTP 429). Then extract the frames at the nav-row seconds with ffmpeg. For tape-based CA surveys download the `vertical` camera first, `oblique` second.
- Sampling strategy for a budget of N samples:
  1. Stills first: stratify across the photo-bearing layers in proportion to sqrt(count) (California 78k, Hawaii/Pacific 28k, Massachusetts 8k, Rhode Island 2k, Puget Sound 0.9k, old California 0.4k), then across `video`/cruise (`picasa_album_id` is a good stratum), taking at most about 5 photos per album/tape, spread over the `frame` range.
  2. Videos (once approved): choose distinct `youtube_id`s round-robin across layers/regions and years, one 60–120 s clip per video, frames every 10 s from the nav `frame` grid; skip the first and last 10 s (deck/water column) and any frame next to a position jump.
  3. Always keep `youtube_id`, `video`, `frame`, `picasa_album_id`, `picasa_id`, the layer `typeName` and the row `id` for dedup and for reproducibility.
- Expected scripts/download/usgs_cmgp.sh behaviour: dry-run writes the layer list and nav rows (about 2.5 M rows, several hundred MB as JSON; fetch only the allowlisted layers) to `<data_root>/usgs_cmgp/metadata/`; download writes stills to `images/`, videos (if enabled) to `videos/`.

## Manual steps (human)
- Decide whether the videos may be fetched from YouTube (the content is public domain, but YouTube's terms restrict downloading and the first scripted watch-page request returned HTTP 429). Without that decision the pipeline can still ingest the roughly 117,600 stills. If the answer is no, ask the USGS PCMSC data coordinator (pcmsc_data@usgs.gov) whether the original video files can be shared (this contact is quoted from the USGS metadata; do not put it in any request).
- One-off check of the allowlist against the layers shown in the portal (the fa-layer to area mapping above was inferred from extents).

## Short download instruction
1. Enumerate the allowlisted underwater layers with the WFS at data.axds.co/gs (JSON, sortBy=id, startIndex paging, use properties.lat/lon).
2. Stills: GET servomatic9000.axiomalaska.com/photo-server/usgs/<picasa_album_id>/<picasa_id>/photo? (no Range support).
3. Videos exist only on YouTube (channel CMG Video, `youtube_id`): needs a human go/ no-go on YouTube terms before fetching.
4. Geo = nav row lat/lon; video frames are interpolated on `frame` seconds (segment). Exclude aerial layers.

## Open questions / risks
- YouTube terms / 429 for scripted access to the only video copy; the stills host (servomatic9000.axiomalaska.com) is legacy Axiom infrastructure and could vanish without notice (the portal moved to video.ioos.us; the DOI target changed).
- The WFS tables hold no capture date for the California layer; getting dates needs the frame service or cruise-ID parsing. Time zone of `time`/`utc` columns in other layers was not verified.
- Counts of distinct videos and total video hours per layer were not measured (only row counts and the provider's totals); the layer-to-area mapping of the `fa*` layers is inferred.
- Some navigation rows are wrong (3.7 km jump in 2 s). No QC flags are provided, so apply a speed filter.
- Rows far from the nominal layer region (`videopoints_hw`, 8 N) show layers are not purely regional.
- Possibly non-USGS content in the portal (CUIA from CSU Monterey Bay; "IOOS partner agencies"): licence not read, kept out.
- Stability: portal data is "prototype" per its own disclaimer; photo ids may be rewritten by Axiom.
- Overlap with USGS ScienceBase releases that hold some of the same frames and navigation (for example Faga'alu Bay frame grabs, Oregon OCS 2014-607-FA, DS 735 Hawaii), which may give original-quality files; not investigated.
